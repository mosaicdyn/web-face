November 2026

November 23, 2026Maps

Cobblestone clean-up after its return

  • Cobblestone: closed a spot in the Princess tower where the bomb could be thrown out of reach.
  • Cobblestone: fixed bad wallbangs, boosts, walkable ledges and gaps, plus smoother movement across the map.
  • Cobblestone: grenades no longer fall through the world at one spot on B.
  • Third-person silent reload animations now play at the right speed.

What it means for you: Cobblestone is new in matchmaking again, so expect more fixes like these for a while.

November 19, 2026MapsGrenades

Inferno B grenade exploit closed, more Cobblestone fixes

  • Inferno: closed a grenade and molotov exploit spot from Church to Dark on B site.
  • Cobblestone: fixed walkable ledges in Long A, an empty skybox, an audio blocker near mid/A, and added a grenade clip to the van bumper.
  • New slow (silent) reload animation for the AWP.
  • Players in Switzerland now open containers through an X-Ray Scanner in the inventory.

What it means for you: If you used a throw from Church into Dark on Inferno B, test it again in practice before you rely on it.

October 2026

October 28, 2026Gameplay

Silent reload: hold or toggle

  • New setting: silent reload can work as Hold or Toggle. With Toggle you can let go of the key once it starts; tap reload again to switch back to a fast reload.
  • The first sound of a reload no longer leaks when you asked for a silent one.
  • Fixed liquid rendering black on AMD cards and some demo-rewind bugs.

What it means for you: If holding reload in a clutch feels awkward, switch silent reload to Toggle.

October 22, 2026Workshop

  • Radar elements can now appear in every game mode, not only Rush.
  • Script warnings are more detailed and show the call stack.

September 23, 2026RushCrosshair

Rush timing changes and crosshairs in pixels

  • Rush: the countdown after an elimination grows from 7 to 14 seconds in end rooms and on Convoy, and does not start when a team dies to suicides, team kills or disconnects; warmup, freeze time and timeouts are shorter.
  • Crosshair thickness, length and gap are now set in pixels and scale with your resolution automatically.
  • New half-outline option and a static square style; dynamic styles use a 64-pixel baseline for spread.
  • Clan tags show in the kill feed.

What it means for you: Your crosshair numbers now mean pixels — check your crosshair again after changing resolution or loading an old setup.

September 22, 2026PremierGameplayCrosshairMapsGrenades

New Premier map pick, Rush mode, silent reload and new crosshairs

  • Premier map choice changed: Team 1 picks a map, Team 2 picks two, Team 1 picks one more, and one of the four is drawn at random.
  • New 3v3 mode, Rush: fight through random arenas to the enemy castle and hold the tower to win the round.
  • Hold reload to reload silently but slowly; un-scoping a sniper is silent; bullet impact decals are clearer.
  • ammo_grenade_limit_total now also limits how many grenades you can buy in a round.
  • Crosshairs no longer depend on resolution, live in a new Crosshair/Scopes tab and default to the Dynamic Quad style.
  • Map fixes on Ancient (grenade clipping in Jaguar), Cache, Mirage and Train; clan tags for Steam groups and stickers on the C4.

What it means for you: You will play the maps your team picked more often in Premier, so a small map pool you know well pays off even more.

Questions

When was the last CS2 update?

The latest update on this page is from November 23, 2026: cobblestone clean-up after its return. We add new updates at the top of this page.

Which maps are in the CS2 Active Duty pool?

Since Premier Season Five began on July 8, 2026: Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage and Nuke. Cache came in and Overpass went out with that update.

Can a CS2 update break my grenade lineups?

Yes. Clipping changes, new geometry or a change in player height can move where a grenade lands. In 2026 Valve warned that lineups thrown from slopes may have changed with the new animation system, and Cache’s grenade clipping changed several times. Re-test a lineup in practice after a big map update.

Where can I read the official CS2 patch notes?

Valve posts every update on the Counter-Strike 2 news page on Steam. Each entry on this page links to its official post.
