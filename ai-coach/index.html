What the AI coach helps with

  • Tactics and setups. It explains why a tactic works and when to use it.
  • Decisions. Rotate or hold, save or retake, peek or wait — talk through the situation.
  • Your playstyle. Tell it your role and level, and it adapts the advice to you.
  • Progress. Turn a goal like “reach FACEIT level 10” into steps you can practise.

FACEIT level 10 starts at 2001 Elo. The AI coach works next to the lineups, tactics and setups in the app, so advice turns straight into something you can practise.

The AI coach gives advice, not guarantees. Check important settings and lineups in a practice match before you rely on them.

Questions

What can I ask the RushB AI coach?

Anything about CS2: tactics, positions, utility, settings, demos, your mistakes and how to climb on FACEIT or in Premier.

Does the AI coach watch my matches?

No. You describe the situation or your problem in the chat, and the coach answers based on that.

Is the AI coach free?

The app is free to download. Access to some features, including parts of the AI coach, may require RushB Pro.
