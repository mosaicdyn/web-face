Frequently asked questions

What is RushB?

RushB is a CS2 companion app: 2000+ single and combo grenade lineups, 100+ tactics, 200+ solo and duo setups, binds and console commands, and an AI coach — in one app for iPhone and Android.

Is RushB free?

Yes, RushB is free to download. Some content and features are part of the RushB Pro subscription, which you can buy in the app.

Which devices does RushB work on?

iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). A web app is coming soon.

Can I buy RushB Pro on the website?

Not yet. Subscriptions are bought and managed inside the app through the App Store or Google Play.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RushB → Cancel Subscription.

Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RushB → Cancel.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is RushB a cheat? Can I get banned for using it?

No. RushB is a guide: it does not run alongside the game and does not change game files. You learn the lineups and tactics in the app and use them yourself.

Is RushB affiliated with Valve or FACEIT?

No. RushB is an independent project and is not affiliated with Valve, FACEIT or other game publishers.

How old do I need to be to use RushB?

You must be at least 13 years old, as stated in our Terms of Use.

How do I contact support?

Email support@therushb.com or use the contact form.
