Why we made RushB

Most players don’t lose because of their aim. They lose because they don’t know the lineups, the tactics and the setups that higher-level players use every round. That knowledge is spread across hundreds of videos and old forum posts.

RushB puts it in one app: grenade lineups for every competitive map, tactics for every round type, setups for solo players and duos, binds and console commands, and an AI coach that answers your questions. Our goal is simple — help players climb, from their first FACEIT matches to level 10.

Who we are

RushB is built by a small team of Counter-Strike players, developers and designers. The app is published by Mosaic Dynamics LTD.

Independent project

RushB is not affiliated with Valve, FACEIT or other game publishers. Counter-Strike and CS2 are trademarks of Valve Corporation.
