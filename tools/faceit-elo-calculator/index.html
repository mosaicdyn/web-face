How the estimate works

FACEIT has not published its exact formula. What it does say is that the Elo you win or lose depends on how likely your team was to win: beat a stronger team and you get more, lose to a weaker one and you drop more. Between evenly matched teams a match moves you about 25 Elo, which is the default here.

So the calculator works with averages: your net Elo per match is win rate × Elo per win − loss rate × Elo per loss, and the matches to your goal are the Elo you still need divided by that number. At 55% and 25 Elo per match that is about 2.5 Elo per match — which is why the climb feels slow, and why your win rate matters more than anything else.

What the calculator does not count

  • Placement matches. The first 10 matches of a season move your Elo more than usual.
  • Your own performance. For Levels 1–9, FACEIT adds a small bonus or penalty for individual impact, on top of the result.
  • Leavers. If a teammate leaves and you still win, you get the normal Elo and the leaver only half.

Questions

What Elo is FACEIT Level 10?

Level 10 starts at 2,001 Elo and has no upper limit. Level 9 is 1,751–2,000 Elo.

How much Elo do you get per win on FACEIT?

About 25 in a match between evenly rated teams. You get more for beating a stronger team and less for beating a weaker one, and the same goes for losses in reverse.

How many matches does it take to reach Level 10?

It depends on your win rate. From 1,200 Elo you need 801 Elo: about 321 matches at a 55% win rate, or about 161 at 60%. Use the calculator above with your own numbers.

Does K/D affect FACEIT Elo?

Only a little. The match result decides most of it; for Levels 1–9 FACEIT adds a small amount for individual impact.

What Elo do new FACEIT players start with?

If you have Prime and enough Premier wins, FACEIT converts your latest Premier rating into Elo. Otherwise you start at 1,000 Elo.
