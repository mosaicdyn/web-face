How to use CS2 tactics in your matches

Most matches are lost before the first duel: five players peek one by one, without utility and without a plan. A tactic gives every player a job and a timing.

  1. Pick the round type. A full-buy execute needs other tools than a pistol round or an eco.
  2. Read the roles. Each tactic says who throws which grenade and who enters first.
  3. Learn the utility. Open the smokes and flashes of the tactic in the lineups section and practise them.
  4. Call it before the round. One short call is enough when everyone knows the plan.

Tactics for duos and small stacks

You do not need a full five-stack. Many tactics work for two or three players who queue together; for those, see solo and duo setups.
