Why setups matter

A crossfire turns a fair duel into a two-on-one. When two players hold different angles of the same entrance, the enemy can only look at one of them — and the other gets the kill.

How to use a duo setup

  1. Agree on the setup before the round starts.
  2. Take your positions and check that your angles cover each other.
  3. Use the utility of the setup to slow the enemy and force them into the crossfire.
  4. Trade: if your partner dies, the setup tells you where to swing.

In the app, solo, duo and triple setups are sorted by A site, mid and B site, and each one has a description and a video. Setups use the same grenades as the lineups section, and for full-team plans see tactics.
