Questions

Are these Inferno lineups free?

Yes. The 71 lineups on this page are free to watch. RushB has 170 Inferno lineups in total — the rest, and the combos, are in the app with RushB Pro.

How do I practise Inferno smokes?

Start an offline match on Inferno with unlimited nades and the grenade camera, then repeat each throw with sv_rethrow_last_grenade. The full setup is in our guide how to practice nades in CS2.

Is Inferno in the CS2 map pool?

Yes. Inferno is one of the seven maps of the Active Duty pool, which is used in Premier and at Majors.
