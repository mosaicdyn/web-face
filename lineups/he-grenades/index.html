What an HE grenade is for

An HE grenade deals damage in an area and briefly opens a hole in a smoke. It rarely kills on its own, but it sets up the fight: a damaged defender loses duels they would otherwise win.

Ways to use HE grenades

  • Stack damage with your teammates’ grenades on common defender positions.
  • Finish low players after a trade instead of peeking them.
  • Stop a rush on a narrow chokepoint early in the round.
  • Open a smoke for a moment to catch players crossing behind it.

Tips for HE lineups

  1. Throw at the positions defenders hold most often — the lineup tells you where.
  2. Time two HE grenades together for more damage on the same spot.
  3. Use sound and information first: an HE on an empty spot is a wasted $300.

Questions

Is an HE grenade worth buying?

Yes, when it has a target: common defender positions, low players after a trade or a narrow chokepoint during a rush.

Does an HE grenade clear a smoke?

The explosion opens a hole in a smoke for a short time. Use it to catch players crossing behind the smoke.
