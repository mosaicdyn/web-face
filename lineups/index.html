Single and combo nades

Every map in RushB has two modes. Switch between them at the top of the map screen.

Single

One grenade, one lineup

Tap a grenade on the radar to see its throw: where to stand, where to aim and how to throw it — for example running with a left click. A screenshot breakdown starts from the start position, and a video shows the full throw.

Combo

A set of grenades that work together

A combo groups the nades for one play — for example two smokes, a flash and an HE for a B site take. Tap any grenade or player on the radar to open the combo and see every nade in it.

How to practise nades faster

Learn a lineup in a private match before you use it in Premier or on FACEIT. Start an offline game, open the console and paste this practice config:

sv_cheats 1
sv_infinite_ammo 1
ammo_grenade_limit_total 5
sv_grenade_trajectory_prac_pipreview 1
sv_grenade_trajectory_prac_trailtime 8
mp_buy_anywhere 1
mp_buytime 9999
mp_freezetime 0
mp_roundtime_defuse 60
mp_warmup_end
bind n sv_rethrow_last_grenade

Now you have unlimited grenades, a camera that shows where each throw will land, and N repeats your last grenade without walking back to the spot. Every command, the binds and a practice routine are in our guide how to practice nades in CS2.

About jump-throw binds. Since the August 2024 update, binds that combine more than one movement or attack action (for example a jump-throw or null bind) no longer work on Valve servers. Valve made the jump-throw reliable without a bind: jump and release the grenade at the same time. A single-action bind such as bind mwheelup +jump is still fine.

Questions

What is a lineup in CS2?

A lineup is a fixed position and aim point that lands a grenade on the same spot every time. Learning a few lineups per map lets you smoke, flash or molotov a position without guessing.

How many lineups are in RushB?

More than 2000 smoke, flash, HE and molotov lineups for the competitive maps — single nades, combos and multi-throw variations from one spot.

What is the difference between single and combo nades?

A single nade is one lineup: one grenade with its position, aim point, throw type, screenshots and video. A combo is a set of grenades that work together for one play — for example two smokes, a flash and an HE for a site take — so you see every nade of the play in one place.

Do I need a jump-throw bind?

No. Since August 2024 Valve blocks binds that combine several movement or attack actions on its servers, and the jump-throw is reliable without a bind. Jump and release the grenade at the same moment.
