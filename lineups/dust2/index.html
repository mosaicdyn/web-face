Questions

Are these Dust 2 lineups free?

Yes. The 121 lineups on this page are free to watch. RushB has 243 Dust 2 lineups in total — the rest, and the combos, are in the app with RushB Pro.

How do I practise Dust 2 smokes?

Start an offline match on Dust 2 with unlimited nades and the grenade camera, then repeat each throw with sv_rethrow_last_grenade. The full setup is in our guide how to practice nades in CS2.

Is Dust 2 in the CS2 map pool?

Yes. Dust 2 is one of the seven maps of the Active Duty pool, which is used in Premier and at Majors.
