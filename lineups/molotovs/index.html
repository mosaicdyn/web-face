Molotov and incendiary grenades

Terrorists buy the molotov and CTs buy the incendiary grenade; both cover an area in fire for a few seconds. Fire forces players out of close corners, delays pushes and buys time — and a smoke grenade puts the fire out.

Ways to use molotovs

  • Clear close corners on a site before your team walks in.
  • Stop a push through a chokepoint and gain time for a rotation.
  • Post-plant molotovs on the bomb to delay or deny the defuse.
  • Retake molotovs to flush players out of their positions.

Tips for molotov lineups

  1. Learn one post-plant molotov per bombsite you play on most.
  2. Throw it from a safe spot — a good lineup does not make you peek.
  3. Remember that an enemy smoke can put your fire out.

Questions

What is the difference between a molotov and an incendiary?

Terrorists buy the molotov and CTs buy the incendiary grenade. Both cover an area in fire for a few seconds.

Can a smoke put out a molotov?

Yes. A smoke grenade extinguishes the fire, so a post-plant molotov can be countered with a smoke on the bomb.
