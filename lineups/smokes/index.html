What smokes do in CS2

Smokes in CS2 are volumetric: the cloud fills the space it lands in, reacts to light and can be opened for a moment by gunfire or an HE grenade. A good smoke blocks a line of sight long enough for your team to cross, plant or retake.

Types of smoke lineups

  • Execute smokes cut the defenders’ angles when your team takes a bombsite.
  • Cross and mid smokes let you cross an open area or split the map.
  • One-way smokes let you see an enemy’s legs or body while they cannot see you.
  • Instant smokes are thrown from spawn or early positions to land before the enemy arrives.
  • Retake smokes isolate one angle so CTs can retake the site one fight at a time.

How to throw a smoke lineup

  1. Stand in the exact position — corners, edges and textures are your reference points.
  2. Aim at the marked point on the wall, sky or object.
  3. Use the throw type the lineup asks for: normal throw, jump-throw, run-throw or a right-click lob.
  4. Check the landing in a practice match first, then use it in Premier or FACEIT.

About jump-throw binds. Since the August 2024 update, binds that combine more than one movement or attack action (for example a jump-throw or null bind) no longer work on Valve servers. Valve made the jump-throw reliable without a bind: jump and release the grenade at the same time. A single-action bind such as bind mwheelup +jump is still fine.

Questions

What is a one-way smoke?

A smoke placed so that you can see an enemy (often their legs or body) while they cannot see you through the cloud.

Why doesn’t my smoke land in the same spot?

Your position, aim point or throw type is slightly off. Practise with sv_rethrow_last_grenade and the trajectory preview in an offline match.

Can you shoot through smokes in CS2?

Bullets and HE grenades open a smoke for a moment, so a smoke is not a perfect wall. Combine it with flashes and timing.
