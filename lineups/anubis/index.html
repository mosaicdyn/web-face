Questions

Are these Anubis lineups free?

Yes. The 31 lineups on this page are free to watch. RushB has 70 Anubis lineups in total — the rest, and the combos, are in the app with RushB Pro.

How do I practise Anubis smokes?

Start an offline match on Anubis with unlimited nades and the grenade camera, then repeat each throw with sv_rethrow_last_grenade. The full setup is in our guide how to practice nades in CS2.

Is Anubis in the CS2 map pool?

Yes. Anubis is one of the seven maps of the Active Duty pool, which is used in Premier and at Majors.
