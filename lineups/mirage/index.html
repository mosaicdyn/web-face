Questions

Are these Mirage lineups free?

Yes. The 91 lineups on this page are free to watch. RushB has 160 Mirage lineups in total — the rest, and the combos, are in the app with RushB Pro.

How do I practise Mirage smokes?

Start an offline match on Mirage with unlimited nades and the grenade camera, then repeat each throw with sv_rethrow_last_grenade. The full setup is in our guide how to practice nades in CS2.

Is Mirage in the CS2 map pool?

Yes. Mirage is one of the seven maps of the Active Duty pool, which is used in Premier and at Majors.
