PRIVACY POLICY

RushB - CS2 Guide

Last updated: 16.12.2025

RushB (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a mobile application that provides educational and entertainment content for Counter-Strike 2 players, including grenade guides, tactics, duo setups, retakes, and AI-powered recommendations. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have. By using RushB, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

🔹 1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information that directly identifies you (such as your name, email, phone number, or account details), because RushB does not have user accounts. However, we collect and process certain technical and usage data required to operate the app and improve user experience.

1.1. Automatically collected data

Through SDKs and analytics tools, we may collect:

  • Device information (device model, OS version, screen size, language);
  • App usage events
    (e.g., which grenades, tactics, or guides were viewed);
  • Subscription status
    (active, expired, cancelled);
  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — if permission is granted;
  • Crash reports and performance data;
  • In-app behaviour analytics (e.g., which features are used);

This data is collected without linking it to your identity.

1.2. Data collected by third-party services

RushB uses several SDKs that may collect data independently:

  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics);
  • Firebase Remote Config (feature configuration);
  • AdMob (Google) (ads personalization and frequency capping);
  • AppsFlyer (attribution and install analytics);
  • Amplitude (product analytics);
  • OneSignal (push notifications);
  • Adapty (subscription management);

These services may collect data such as device identifiers, IP address (short-term), interactions with ads, install source, and in-app behaviour. Each service processes data under its own Privacy Policy.

🔹 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data only for:

  • Ensuring the correct technical operation of the app;
  • Improving content relevance (e.g., most-used maps);
  • Personalizing user experience;
  • Attribution and performance measurement of advertising campaigns;
  • Managing subscriptions and paywalls;
  • Sending push notifications you opted into;
  • Preventing abuse and improving security;
  • Displaying ads (AdMob) and limiting repetition;

We never sell personal data.

🔹 3. Advertising

RushB displays ads inside the app using Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers (including IDFA if permission granted);
  • IP address (temporary);
  • interactions with ads;
  • performance metrics;

Users can disable personalized ads at any time through iOS Settings (“Limit Ad Tracking” / App Tracking Transparency). If you decline tracking, ads may still appear, but they will not be personalized.

🔹 4. Subscriptions and Payments

All purchases in RushB are processed exclusively via Apple App Store (StoreKit).

We do not collect or store:

  • credit card numbers;
  • payment details;
  • billing information;

Apple manages all payments, renewals, refunds, and billing issues. Your subscription status is processed only to unlock content inside the app.

🔹 5. Children’s Privacy

RushB is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that data was collected from a child, we will delete it.

Third-Party Access

Since the Application does not collect user data, no information is shared with third-party entities or services.

🔹 6. Data Retention

We retain usage data only for as long as needed for app analytics, improvements, and security. You may request deletion of data stored by third-party providers (see Section 9).

🔹 7. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • companies providing analytics, attribution, or monetization SDKs;
  • infrastructure providers hosting our servers (for content delivery).

We never sell your data.

🔹 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

🔹 9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your data;
  • request deletion;
  • opt out of personalized ads;
  • withdraw consent for tracking;
  • request correction or restriction;

Requests may be sent to our support email.
